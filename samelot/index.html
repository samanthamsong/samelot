TODAY'S TRANSLATION QUEST

A quiet morning

Every morning, I drink coffee by the window. I watch the rain and write a short note to my friend. Then I walk to the market to buy bread and fruit.

Word help
  • every morning = chaque matin
  • by the window = près de la fenêtre
  • to watch = regarder
  • rain = la pluie
  • a short note = un petit mot
  • then = ensuite / puis
  • market = le marché
